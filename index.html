About This Site

⚡ The Weather Side

Personal weather tracking for North Georgia. Live data comes straight from the National Weather Service API. I'm a trained-eye storm watcher, not a meteorologist — when severe weather threatens, trust your local NWS warnings first. This site is my own report, for my own community.

🌿 The Wildlife Side

Everything here is what actually shows up in the yard. Feeders, trees, and a camera pointed at the good stuff. Photos are mine, taken from the porch — nobody gets chased, nobody gets harassed. Just documentation of the neighborhood.
